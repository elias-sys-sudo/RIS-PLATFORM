# RIS Platform — User Guide

## For Suppliers

### Getting Started
1. Visit the RIS Platform and click "Check your eligibility"
2. Complete the registration wizard (company details, KYC documents)
3. Wait for credit officer approval
4. Once approved, log in to submit invoices

### Submitting an Invoice
1. Go to Invoices > New Invoice
2. Select or request a buyer
3. Enter face value, issue date, due date
4. Click "Submit Invoice"
5. Your invoice will be reviewed by a credit officer

### Tracking Your Invoice
Your invoice moves through these stages:
- **Submitted** — under review
- **Buyer Confirmed** — buyer verified
- **Scored** — risk assessed
- **Priced** — discount rate set
- **Approved** — ready for funding
- **Approved** — accepted, pending payment authorisation
- **Pending First Auth** — first finance manager has authorised
- **Pending Second Auth** — awaiting second finance manager (dual-auth)
- **Executing** — payment being processed
- **Funded** — money disbursed to your account
- **Collecting** — RIS collecting from buyer
- **Collected** — buyer has paid in full
- **Settled** — profit booked, lifecycle complete

## For Credit Officers

### Daily Workflow
1. Check **Invoices** for new submissions
2. Confirm buyers, run risk scoring, generate pricing
3. Review **Approvals** queue — approve or reject
4. Monitor **Buyer Requests** from suppliers
5. Check **Collections** for overdue items

## For Finance Managers

### Payment Authorisation
RIS requires two different finance managers to authorise each payment:
1. First manager clicks "Authorise Payment"
2. Second manager (different person) clicks "Second Authorisation"
3. Click "Execute Payment" to initiate disbursement
4. Click "Mark as Funded" to confirm payment sent

### Collections
- Monitor the Collections page for outstanding items
- Record payments as they come in
- The system auto-marks as "Collected" when outstanding reaches zero

### Settlements
- When a collection is fully paid, a settlement is automatically created
- Navigate to **Settlements** to see profit booking details
- Settlement shows: face value, advance amount, discount earned, net profit
- Status progresses: pending → facility_repaid → profit_booked → closed

# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors, regardless of experience level, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.

## Our Standards

**Positive behaviors:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the project
- Showing empathy towards other contributors

**Unacceptable behaviors:**
- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated, and will result in a response deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.

I am building RIS — an Invoice Discounting and Early Payment platform operating in Uganda. RIS pays suppliers within 72 hours by purchasing approved invoices at a discount, then collecting full payment from buyers at maturity. We operate under Bank of Uganda fintech regulations, AML/KYC compliance, and Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019.  Tech stack: Node.js 20 LTS, TypeScript (strict mode), Express, PostgreSQL 15, Redis, Bull queue, React 18, Docker. Payment rails: MTN Mobile Money API, Airtel Money API, Uganda EFT/RTGS for large transfers.  We follow: SOLID principles, OWASP Top 10 (2021), ISO/IEC 25010 quality, ISO 27001 security, IEEE 829 testing, OpenAPI 3.0, 12-Factor App, and shift-left security (security in every phase, not just one). Every action writes to an immutable audit log. All payments require dual authorisation. All financial records retained for 7 years minimum.  The system has 11 modules: Auth, Onboarding (KYC/KYB), Invoice Intake, Buyer Verification & Assignment, Risk Scoring Engine, Pricing Engine, Approval Workflow, Payment Engine, Collections Tracking, Bank Facility Management, and Reporting.
